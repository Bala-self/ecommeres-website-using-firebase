For all Cash on Delivery orders:

Customers are responsible for providing an accurate physical address, valid landmark, and active telephone number. Our courier partners make up to two (2) delivery attempts. Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days from the date of confirmed delivery if the item qualifies under our Return Policy (e.g. damaged, wrong item, defective, or missing components).

12. Return Conditions

To qualify for a valid return:

Section 02 · Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 23 September 2026

At Kadai Retail E-Commerce (“Kadai”, “we”, “us”), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the personal information strictly necessary to process your orders and deliver dependable service:

  • Identity & Contact: Full name, telephone/mobile number, email address.
  • Delivery Information: Physical shipping address, landmark, city, state, and 6-digit PIN code.
  • Order History: Products purchased, quantities, order status, applied discount coupons, and delivery timelines.
  • Customer Inquiries: Communications with our support team regarding order queries, returns, or feedback.
  • Technical Telemetry: Anonymized browser type, operating system, and IP address collected automatically by our cloud hosting infrastructure for fraud prevention and system security.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for legitimate retail operations:

  • Processing, packaging, and dispatching your orders.
  • Communicating order status updates and placing verification phone calls for Cash on Delivery orders.
  • Providing responsive customer support, resolving delivery issues, and facilitating returns/refunds.
  • Preventing fraudulent, bot-driven, or abusive order placement.
  • Fulfilling legal, statutory, and tax compliance obligations under Indian law.

No Financial Card Credentials Collected

Because Kadai utilizes a Cash on Delivery model, we never ask for, collect, or store your credit card numbers, debit card details, CVVs, or internet banking passwords on our website.

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to any third-party marketing firms or brokers. We disclose your data only to trusted operational service providers bound by strict confidentiality obligations:

  • Logistics & Courier Partners: Name, delivery address, and telephone number shared strictly for completing parcel delivery.
  • Backend Cloud Infrastructure: Secure cloud data hosting via Google Firebase located in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region.
  • Law Enforcement & Legal Authorities: Only when strictly required by a court order, summons, or applicable statutory regulation under Indian law.

4. Data Security

We implement robust technical and organizational measures, including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, strict role-based access control, and hardened cloud security rules, to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

5. Data Retention

We retain personal information for the period required to fulfill your orders, manage return records, resolve disputes, and maintain statutory tax accounting records as mandated by Indian business regulations.

6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal information associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated contact or address information.
  • Request account deactivation or deletion of personal data where not required for active tax or legal defense obligations.

To exercise your privacy rights, email our Privacy Desk at privacy@kadai.in.

Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: 23 September 2026

7-Day Return Guarantee

Every purchase on Kadai is backed by our straightforward 7-Day Return Guarantee. If your item arrives damaged, defective, or not as described, we will arrange a replacement or refund.

Eligibility Matrix

Reason for Return Eligibility Status Required Documentation
Wrong product delivered Eligible Photo of received package & invoice

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact us within 7 days of delivery at support@kadai.in or call +91 98765 43210.
  2. Provide your Order Number, registered phone number, and a brief description of the issue along with photographs or an unboxing video.
  3. Our support team will verify your claim within 24 hours and arrange reverse pickup or provide courier return instructions.
  4. Ensure the item is safely packed in its original carton with all tags, accessories, and user documentation intact.

Refund Processing

Once the returned item arrives at our Chennai fulfillment center, it undergoes standard quality inspection within 48 hours.

  • For Cash on Delivery Orders: Our accounts team will request your preferred bank account details (Account Number, IFSC, Account Holder Name) or UPI ID. Refunds are credited via direct IMPS/NEFT transfer within 3 to 5 business days of approval.
  • Replacement Option: Where stock is readily available, we are pleased to dispatch a brand-new replacement unit at no additional cost.
